Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What age is suitable to start Quran classes? ▾

We welcome children as young as 4 years old. Our beginner classes use playful, visual methods to introduce Arabic letters in a way young learners enjoy. There is no upper age limit — it's never too late to start!

What platform do the online classes take place on? ▾

All lessons are conducted live via Zoom or Skype. You'll need a device with a camera and microphone — a tablet, laptop, or PC all work well. We send a digital whiteboard link for interactive learning activities.

What if my child doesn't know any Arabic? ▾

That's perfectly fine! Our Beginner course starts from absolute zero using the Noorani Qaida — a time-tested method used worldwide. Most children can begin reading simple Arabic words within just a few weeks.

Can I reschedule or cancel a lesson? ▾

Yes. We understand that family life can be unpredictable. You can reschedule any lesson with at least 12 hours' notice at no extra cost. We offer flexible morning, afternoon, and evening slots across multiple time zones.

Is the teacher background-checked and safe for children? ▾

Absolutely. Our teacher holds a verified certification and has undergone a thorough background check. All lessons are one-on-one with a parent or guardian always welcome to sit in. Child safety is our highest priority.
